About MUN Cyber Technologies

Building useful cybersecurity technology from the ground up.

MUN Cyber Technologies is a technology company focused on cybersecurity awareness, software development, and defensive cyber systems. We are interested in the intersection of cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, automation, and practical security operations.

Our goal is to develop solutions that make security work more understandable, efficient, and actionable while maintaining a strong focus on responsible defensive use.
